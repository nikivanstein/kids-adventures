title: Why this site exists
---

A while ago I started playing little D&D-style adventures with my two sons, aged 3 and 5 — using their own toys as the set. No dice, no rules, just a story where they make the choices: which way do we go, which tool do we grab, how do we solve this together?

It works surprisingly well. Bit by bit, playing this way teaches them to solve problems, to plan ahead, and — maybe most importantly — to work together. And it gives their imagination all the room it needs: there's no wrong answer, just a story that bends around whatever they come up with.

This site exists because I wanted to save these mini-adventures and share them easily — with you, and with anyone else who enjoys them. Every adventure is free, bilingual (Dutch and English), and grouped into worlds and campaigns so new stories can keep being added.
