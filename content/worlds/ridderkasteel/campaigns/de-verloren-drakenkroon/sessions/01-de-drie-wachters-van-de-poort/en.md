**Two roles:** the **Knight** speaks to the guardians, the **Squire** carries and chooses the right item.

---

## Introduction

> "At the castle gate stand three tall, silent guardians. As you approach, the middle one speaks in a deep, echoing voice: 'None may pass who cannot prove their worth. Show us courage, light, and a call for help — and the gate shall open.'"

**The Knight's first choice:** "Which guardian shall we speak to first — the one on the left, in the middle, or on the right?"

---

## The First Guardian: Courage

> "The first guardian points to your little shield. 'Show me you're not afraid,' he rumbles."

**Choice moment (Squire):** "Which item shows courage?" → **little shield**.

> "You raise the little shield high. The guardian nods slowly and steps aside."

---

## The Second Guardian: Light

> "The second guardian gestures to the dark passage behind him. 'Light my way,' he says."

**Choice moment:** "Which item could light the way?" → **candle**.

> "You hold up the candle. A warm glow fills the passage, and the guardian steps aside too."

---

## The Third Guardian: The Call

> "The last guardian crosses his arms. 'Call for help, so the whole castle may hear you're here.'"

**Choice moment:** "What could call for help?" → **horn**.

> "You blow the horn — a bright, echoing note rings out across the courtyard. The third guardian smiles and steps aside."

---

## Finale: Through the Gate

> "The great gate creaks open. Beyond it, torchlight flickers over a courtyard — somewhere in there, the Lost Dragon Crown is waiting to be found. 'Well done,' the guardians say together. 'You may enter, brave ones.'"

---

## Extra tips
- Let the child decide the order the guardians are visited in — the story simply continues from wherever they choose.
- This session sets up the rest of the "Lost Dragon Crown" campaign; the crown itself is found in a later adventure.
