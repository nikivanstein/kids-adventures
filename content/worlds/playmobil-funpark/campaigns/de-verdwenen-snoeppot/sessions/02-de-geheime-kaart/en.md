**Two roles, both equally important:** the **Explorer** picks the tool, the **Tracker** points the way.

---

## Introduction

> "Gnome Pim has another secret: somewhere in FunPark, an old map is hidden, showing even more spots where he once stashed candy. 'If you find that map,' he says, 'I'll never lose anything again!'"

**The Tracker's first choice:** "Shall we start at the thorny hedge, or by the river?"

---

## Location 1: The Overgrown Hedge

> "Ahead of the path stands a hedge full of thick vines — there's no getting through."

**Choice moment (Explorer):** "Which tool could help here?" → **little sword**.

> "With the little sword you carefully cut a path through the vines. Behind it lies a piece of the map!"

---

## Location 2: The Wobbly Bridge

> "Over the 'river' (a blue towel) lies a plank that wobbles loose."

**Choice moment:** "What could tie this plank down?" → **rope**.

> "With the rope you tie the plank in place. Carefully crossing... success! On the other side lies the second piece of the map."

---

## Finale: The Map Complete

> "Together you lay the two map pieces against each other. A route appears to... another hiding spot, somewhere else in FunPark! Gnome Pim jumps up and down: 'Now I'll never lose anything again. Thank you, brave heroes!'"

---

## Extra tips
- A short adventure — great for a quick play session or right before bedtime.
- Let the kids imagine what else might be marked on the map.
