title: Playmobil FunPark
summary: A toy theme park full of adventures, gnomes, roller coasters, and hidden treasure.
---

Playmobil FunPark is the setting for mini-adventures you play out with your own Playmobil figures. Each adventure takes just 20 to 30 minutes and is all about making choices together, following clues, and helping each other out — perfect for young children.
